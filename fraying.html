Fraying At The Altar

Raised in a non-conventionally traditional household, I could witness and join prayers whenever they happened, rather than being coerced into them. There was something genuinely exciting about imagining a powerful being up there, listening to everyone and keeping track. Hands lined up perfectly, clasped. Feet pressed together and glued to the floor. Eyes shut and then ringing Him---or Her up felt seraphic.

Initially, it felt more like reverence than instruction. But spending too much time sitting at the altar taught me this: faith is not rest. It is a tug-of-war.

One part of you wants to give in, to find indefinite comfort in the unity, infiniteness of God and His capability of protection and warmth. The other---constantly, and sometimes inadvertently---wants to rigorously challenge the same. Not merely God's possibility, but existence itself.

After countless late-night conversations with myself that made my thoughts stutter plenty times, I learned that only a mind breathing and throbbing in faith along with questions can explore a godly being. A stale mind weakens the bond; it invokes fear, and superstition instead. To have enough faith that the belief is not insecure, that it will not falter under questioning and reasoning, but rather grow, is what mattered.

My relationship with Him is indeed of such nature, and so it does not scare me to have this conversation. Considering and knowing myself to be the creation, I find solace as I write this essay knowing that I will be held not to trial for judging the might of God and not even punishment but rather given a hearty laugh at most, up there in the heavenly courtroom or something. I am Her burden, just like all her creation, so I might as well give Her a tough time. All of this is possible only when and if I am consciously looking towards Her, trying not to burn up in Her glory and yet uttering out questions even when I have fallen to my knees.

You know what? Let us now make this meta. Hi there. I know you are watching me. Watching me type, delete, and type again. You know what the future is, and I am done with this essay somewhere in it, and yet you let me type it out. I will not question you in each paragraph, because I need time to absorb your answers too, over years if that must be so, but there will be a fair share of those. I am curious in what ways will you let me pose my questions because I know that at the end the answer might not be one but many, not simple, coming from an omnipotent being like you, but rather, maybe extremely convoluted, meant for me to unpack over time. You tell me answers that I do not comprehend, or maybe the truth is sometimes I actively choose not to listen. This is because, well, it just scares me sometimes because it pulls me out of what thoughts comfort me or what is viable for me. Regardless, know that I try my best and that I want to. I will not be whole with conclusions until the end of my journey, until we meet, and for the while we cannot, let us have what seems like horseplay for you, and for me---my most honest questions and grudges.

I wonder if the systems keeping a tree alive wonder what leaf they will let go of first when it is autumn. Is it the oldest one who earned its grace by bending the way the tree wanted it to, to maximise surface looking at the sun, or the youngest one who stretched its apex towards the sun instead, trying to reach it? I've been meaning to ask, why do you let me talk to you? Does it not get repetitive for you? I know infinity is yours, but my time is certain, finite, and burning through; and in this teensy-tiny 'knot' I offer---in a never-ending string of time, I do after all exist and offer at least a little resistance as the string passes through the needle. You must deal with this resistance knowing you are the needle. You are what all my existence must go through, and the thoughts I produce, my sorrow in them, and my gratitude in their reconciliation are all yours at the end. So, why let all of this play out?

Is it because you allow me and my kind free will? You let me question you and do not blind me to reasoning or put a veil between us. You gave me this free will. And in my best sense, I declare that I believe that the most important use you meant for it to be put to was in your consideration, in your meditation, and therefore through you---the world around us, the people in it, and the surroundings. Not because you want praise or woo, nah, but rather because I know there is this bigger something you want us to know.

At the same time, I know that this bigger something is not finite either. It is like a growing sense of awareness. It was you until it was just tectonic plates. It was you until it was just electric discharge from the sky. It was you until it was quantum mechanics. You are always right at the edge of knowledge, the pull, maybe, that keeps us going, and once we reach it, you stray further away and yet right at the edge.

I will agree that you have answered me. I know this not because I suddenly scored well on an exam or because my father bought me what I wanted, but because you let me be. By allowing your plans for me to unfold, by letting me grow, and by giving me a mind capable of understanding what is and what is to follow, you have answered enough of my questions for me to mature into my doubts and recognize them as my own. At the same time, many of your answers must have passed straight through me. I know this because I, too, have said, "Here, this should help you for a month," as I dropped a handful of sugar beside a line of ants---only for them to collect it in weeks, or whatever amount of time they take to pick it up completely. I, too, have gently caressed the leaves of my plants while fertilizing them, whether it stinks or stains my fingers, only for the effects to reveal themselves weeks later. I will never understand much of what you tell me. It is a shame that I may not, but again, I promise to try.

As a child, I imagined that when I prayed, my words would materialize into giant, invisible letters, and float upward into the sky to reach you. There, you would make a list of my questions. And then what? What have you done with the list? I asked, prayed, wished, and prayed again, and while I am certain you heard me, I do not know how many of those were answered by you. This leads me to a possibility: that you revealed those answers to me as I grew and as I acted, and that what now constitutes me is nothing more than those very answers.

I can understand so because, taking into consideration how you are infinite, and I will pick out a segment of this infinity, and in this segment, you chose to birth this world and all in it. You put me in this world you created; creation would mean that it has a beginning, and you gave it one. Beginning, or the start of something, would mean that it did not exist prior to its conception. Conception would in turn mean you had the idea of it in your mind, didn't you? You must have started thinking somewhere. You began somewhere. Now, to understand what you created, you put in your creation, us, sentient beings, and decided to fill us, all of us, with you. You allow us free will to create millions and billions of... you. Each new question asked allows you to answer it. Allows you to see this world you created and be humored by it. I am sure you are like, "Oh ha-ha, right, so that is why I allowed 'e' to be equal to 'mc' squared." Once each thought is thought, each question, through countless lives, questioned, and all of it is answered by you, the world is complete. It has returned to you. It is whole by the virtue of it being you.

To know that I am you, materialized in the form of answers to my questions, makes me feel warm and gives me closure. So, am I nothing but a reflection of you? Built upon your version of seeing this world based on how I questioned you, cemented nicely to see this world through those results. In this way you have tried to experience this world of yours through a quite unique perspective, that is, through my questions.

To realize these answers in countless ways you drive all of us with a purpose---right? Some idea of fate, of destiny? Some push that drives my life into a mould that fits your next puzzle?

Then why is it that you do not reveal me to my purpose? You do not tell me what foot I should place first when I wake up. You do not tell me explicitly what good you will do to me if I give money or food to that one lady at the metro station. You ask me to do good, and I will be rewarded... tomorrow---or wait, was it... next year? No wait---it was in my next life maybe. You guide me and tell me what to do yet keep me from conclusion. Would revelation to a certain extent, at different points in life not help both you and me?

All the questions I ask you endlessly, repetitively, only to sigh them off at the end, satisfied only by the fact that they have hopefully reached you, and comforted by the idea that you might just be listening, I spin around. Instead of using my free will to grab another bag of cookies, I spend it on constantly flooding your letterbox. You let my thoughts wander off to everywhere yet give them no rest. Thus, I question---is your letterbox even real? Is it true that through endless questioning, I can get closer to you?

Fine, I surrender in the sense that not all explanations you give me are those I understand or apply, but I am all too aware of your present and overwhelming silence on a lot of them.

Regardless of the above realization, you sometimes refuse to look at me. All this discourse, and yet I cannot shake the feeling that you are still rather distant. Or am I just too silly and aloof to recognize it? Neither could I be nor do I need to be Jesus to ask you, my god, why have you forsaken me? I know you don't explicitly need to, for you are already aware; hell, I am certain you already know this all too well, but I wish, even if it is through me and along with me, that you would feel the terror of simply feeling unheard.

Not unheard in the I-am-suffering-with-one-eye-open-hoping-for-help-to-arrive, no, instead, like a man running in a desert under a mild sun on pure faith and hope wherein for that long duration he feels your strength knowing he will make it out of this hellscape, he has this confident smirk on his face. He walks with the thought of you lifting his steps, thinking of you guiding him to an end. Yet when after hours of walking he sits down for a break, he sees that the bright yellow sun has not wavered a bit; it is still there, the thumping waves of heat around it still pulsing with much vigor, a huge stretch yet to be covered. And in the silent, monotonous buzz of this vast expanse of nothingness, he realizes that he is indeed just alone. His smirk weakens with each drop of sweat that breaks contact with his skin. He can feel the migraine about to begin due to the temperature. He has no medicine. He realizes that he is indeed just alone.

He could yell oh so loud, and it would not matter in the slightest; he could dig the deepest hole only to find more dry, hot sand; he could dig and pray and dig more only to find more sand. He lets off a funny scoff. It is amusing.

What is he even running for? Where is anything at all? It's all just sand; no sight of help, no water, no telephone. Just sand, and yet, he is alive. He is being kept alive by some force. If not, he would have succumbed to the heat. He knows one thing, though, that being---he knows in his heart that so long as he is alive, he has a purpose. His surroundings, his world, might treat him fairly but also might not, might not show or give anything in the slightest either, and yet he must keep going with no direction to follow simply because the idea of you exists in his head.

Do you see it yet? Do you feel this terror or the exhaustion? Do you also see the extreme faith that drives a soul in the face of this pointlessness? And at the end do you see what terrible friction this face-off can cause and how that ends up changing a person to whatever, given the inimitability of each mind?

You tell me to keep going; sure, I will. Limp in the heat of silence, sure I will keep going. And yeah, it is indeed heat. Not cold, but sulfur. Nothing more than the idea of the existence of your plans for me drives me at this point. But allow me to touch your very broad shoulders for just a second with my unclean hands and shake you ever so slightly because, oh beloved, this is exhausting. It is terrifying, and since it hurts me and many of my kind, I know it hurts you. So why exactly is it that you do let the desert traveller wander?

Call it anti-climactic the way I don't know the answer to this question. I do not understand what use my current exhaustion will be in my next life or my next-to-next life. What use is my suffering if, regardless of my endeavors in it, I do not see meaning in it? What use is it if, instead of seeing explanations, I see more causalities, which prove that my efforts to even mend this might prove wasteful? What purpose will this 'aware' self that I am currently in serve if you let my current state of mind go to waste for a promise of a reveal in my next life when I won't have these questions at all?!

Do you promise to hold and transfer this consciousness? Do you promise to keep my soul alive in all my lives? I do not know. I am not blaming you, for I have not reached a conclusion at all, and presumably it is not deserved yet. How long am I supposed to experiment and isolate conclusions until I find the right one? You only let us all live for, like, 60 years now. And who is to blame if the body leaves mid-journey? I am implying death or a surrender of life. But if I were to engage in it or act it out, given my free will, is it that the lived experience was meant to end there? Was death the answer to it? Are you trying to tell me that death is indeed the answer to certain threads of an unexplained sense of being? Especially when you are the cause of this unexplained-ness. I do not want to believe so. I will end up carrying unexplained questions into my next life if it exists. There's no way I'm falling for that bargain.

Did you realize that when the exhaustion took over the traveller and with half a breath in his lungs he looked up at the sun, he only saw half of it? Because the other half was your shining presence, looking back over your shoulder at him. Was he supposed to take that as motivation and a cosmic pull to continue? Or mockery of his efforts materialized as distance?

Neither.

This honesty or reality of this bond is already the truth of it. This doubt and search for answers is how bond is best described. The urge to run into the desert regardless of consequences is what the bond is. I would not associate myself with you if I knew that you do not know all the answers already, but at the same time I acknowledge the tension that comes with your refusal and your answers, which you reveal whenever you deem fit. Even if that is beyond this lifetime. So long as I think of you deeply, you and I are bound together, even if we are looking away. Never can you truly abandon me, for I am your creation, and even in neglect I lay claimed as yours. This is the strain we will forever share---hah!

I believe in you not because you know the answer to everything but rather because it is you who enables me and gives me a reason to ask these questions in the first place. I realize at the end that our relationship is not based on question → answer or deed → consequence, but rather the intricacies of these actions simply being allowed to occur. Ours is one of reasoning and the complexity that comes with it.

I do not know what comes after this; the tautness of this confusion or mystery stays unresolved. That might just be the beauty of this, for it is, after all, a cosmic relationship; it is grand. And truthfully, I would rather have it this way, because "definiteness," "immediacy," and "God" in the same sentence sound antiparallel.

At the end, while I do respect all faiths, my God is mine to bother. No specific religion, no particular God, just---Him. I might not wake up tomorrow if he is truly upset---I will know for sure!

Morning edit: I woke up. Love you. Eternally yours, even when we're square.
